Player stats

Stat glossary (what do these abbreviations mean?)
BPM
Box Plus/Minus. Estimated points per 100 possessions a player adds versus an average D1 player. Above 0 is better than average; 5+ is excellent.
ORtg / DRtg
Offensive / Defensive Rating. Points produced (ORtg) or allowed (DRtg) per 100 possessions. Higher ORtg is better; lower DRtg is better.
USG%
Usage. Share of the team's possessions a player ends (shot, turnover, free throws) while on the floor.
TS%
True shooting. Scoring efficiency counting 2s, 3s and free throws together.
eFG%
Effective field goal %. Shooting percentage with 3-pointers counted as 1.5 shots.
TOV%
Turnover rate. Turnovers per 100 offensive plays. Lower is better on offense; forcing a high number is better on defense.
ORB%
Offensive rebound rate. Share of available offensive rebounds a team grabs.
FT rate
Free throw attempts per field goal attempt. Shows how often a team gets to the line.
Net / 40
Points scored minus points allowed per 40 minutes while a lineup was on the floor.
